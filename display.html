Acrylic Joy

A bright, playful painting made by Maya to remind little patients that every day can feel like a sunny day.

Back to Gallery

Artist Story

Maya painted this piece with bold colors and gentle hearts after spending time drawing with friends. She wanted to send a message of brightness and comfort to children who need extra hope.

Artwork Details

  • Title: Acrylic Joy
  • Artist: Maya, Grade 7
  • Medium: Acrylic paint on canvas
  • Purpose: Hospital room donation
  • Feelings: Warmth, hope, happiness

Why this matters

Art can calm, inspire, and connect. Each piece donated here is chosen to bring a smile to a child during a hard day.
