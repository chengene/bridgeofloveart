Volunteer Help

Earn service hours while helping children in need through art.

Join our team of volunteers to support art submissions, outreach, packaging, and community events.

What volunteers do

  • Help gather and package donated artwork.
  • Share our mission with schools, families, and social media.
  • Assist with outreach to community partners.
  • Track volunteer hour requests and help document service.

How it works

Fill out the volunteer request form below, and our team will review your availability and match you with the right tasks. Approved volunteers can earn school or community service hours.

  1. Submit your request.
  2. Receive a volunteer assignment.
  3. Complete the task and log your hours.
  4. Get a confirmed volunteer hour record.

Volunteer Hours Request

A team member will review your request and follow up with next steps for volunteer hour approval.

✅ Volunteer request stored locally

Your volunteer request is saved in your browser and will appear on the requests overview page.
